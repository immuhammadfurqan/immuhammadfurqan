## About

I'm an AI Engineer with 2.5 years of focused experience building **RAG systems, LLM applications, and Voice AI agents** for real clients across automotive, education, defense, and enterprise SaaS.

My work bridges research and deployment. I build things that run in production, not just demos. I'm also studying for an MS in Artificial Intelligence at LUMS. I specialize in:

- 🧠 **RAG & LLM Systems**: semantic retrieval, hybrid search, multi-tenant architectures, prompt engineering at scale
- 🎙 **Voice AI Agents**: real-time conversational agents using ElevenLabs, VAPI, Retell AI, Deepgram, and Twilio
- ⚙️ **Backend & APIs**: FastAPI, Django REST, Node.js, AWS, Docker
- ✅ **Evals & guards**: LLM-as-judge and rule-based eval suites, plus server-side checks around every agent action

Open to **AI/ML roles and research collaborations**.
